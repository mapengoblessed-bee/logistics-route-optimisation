Logistics Route Optimization Decision Support System • University Decision Support Systems (DSS) Project

Mathematical Modelling under Certainty • Built with Python, Flask, SQLite & Google OR-Tools

DSS Mathematical Model & System Architecture

1. Problem Formulation: Travelling Salesperson Problem (TSP)

The delivery routing problem is formulated as a classic Travelling Salesperson Problem (TSP) operating under conditions of certainty. In this environment, all customer locations, the distance matrix \(D_{ij}\), the vehicle fuel consumption rate \(F\), and the unit fuel price \(P\) are completely known, fixed, and deterministic.

2. Mathematical Optimization Model

Objective Function:

Minimize Z = Σ(i, j) Cij × Xij

Fuel Cost Calculation per Leg (i, j):

Cij = Dij × (F / 100) × P

Where:

  • Z: Total transportation fuel cost for the complete tour ($).
  • Cij: Transportation fuel cost of travelling directly from location i to location j ($).
  • Dij: Road distance between location i and location j (km).
  • F: Vehicle fuel consumption rate (litres per 100 km).
  • P: Fixed fuel price per litre ($/L).
  • Xij: Binary decision variable where:
    • Xij = 1 if the vehicle travels directly from location i to location j.
    • Xij = 0 otherwise.

Operational Constraints:

  1. Depot Origin & Return: The delivery vehicle departs from the designated depot and must return to the same depot after completing all deliveries.
  2. Single Visit per Destination: Every selected customer delivery location must be visited exactly once:
    Σi Xij = 1   and   Σj Xij = 1  ∀ j.
  3. Subtour Elimination: The tour must form a single connected Hamiltonian cycle with no disjoint sub-loops.
  4. Integrity: Distances and fuel parameters must be strictly non-negative.

3. Solution Method: Google OR-Tools Routing Solver

This system uses Google OR-Tools (pywrapcp.RoutingModel), an industrial-grade combinatorial optimization library.

  • RoutingIndexManager: Manages node indices and handles vehicle origin/destination mappings.
  • Transit Callback: Computes the integer arc cost \(C_{ij}\) in cents for every pair of locations.
  • First Solution Strategy: PATH_CHEAPEST_ARC greedily constructs an initial valid feasible tour.
  • Metaheuristic: GUIDED_LOCAL_SEARCH iteratively escapes local optima to find the globally optimal delivery tour.

4. Role in Decision Support Systems (DSS)

Without an automated DSS, logistics planners rely on intuition or arbitrary routing, leading to excessive fuel consumption, vehicle wear, and carbon emissions. This DSS quantifies the trade-offs, evaluates alternative sequences against the default order, and presents management with verified cost reductions and transparent leg-by-leg plans.
